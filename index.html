Research Focus

World & Foundation Models for Brain Dynamics

  • Latent world models of neural state — learning internal representations that infer, simulate, and forecast how individual brains evolve under external stimuli and interventions
  • Multimodal alignment & reasoning over biosignals — bridging continuous, high-dimensional neural recordings with language and vision via foundation models and LLM post-training
  • Generalizable & clinically grounded NeuroAI — disentangling shared functional structure from individual variability for cross-subject decoding and early disease discovery

Reliable & Long-Horizon AI Agents

  • Interactive simulation & post-training for agents — training and evaluating multi-step, tool-using agents in realistic RL environments with human-in-the-loop user simulation
  • Long-horizon agentic memory — designing memory representations and maintenance mechanisms that sustain coherent reasoning over evolving, long-term context

Yuxiang Wei, Lijing Zhu, Qizhen Lan, Yanteng Zhang, Vince D. Calhoun.

Governs autonomous research agents on NeuroClaimBench (489 executable claims) by separating execution from claim reporting via frozen analysis plans and five deterministic verification gates, cutting false support on confounded controls from 23–38% to 0%.
